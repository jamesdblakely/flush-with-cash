# How to Play Flush with Cash

You inherit one porta-potty and $185. Your job is to turn it into a sustainable business by choosing the right location, keeping the unit operational, and protecting your reputation.

## The daily loop

1. **Choose a site on the town map.** Hover a location to see its traffic color, then select its name, number, or landmark.
2. **Check the permit, condition, and reputation requirements.** The panel below the map explains why a placement is unavailable.
3. **Service the unit if necessary.** Service restores it to 100% condition, but you must still retain enough cash for at least the cheapest permit.
4. **Place the unit.** The weekly calendar shows the current day and opens the street scene.
5. **Watch the operating day.** People can walk by, use the unit, find it occupied, or be turned away. The door marker reads green **VACANT**, red **IN USE**, or orange **CLOSED**.
6. **Review the result.** Revenue minus permits, servicing, upgrades, fines, and supplies is the day’s profit. Choose the next day if the business can still afford a permit.

One operating day lasts 120 simulation minutes, or about 15 seconds at normal speed.

## What to watch

### Cash

Cash pays for permits, servicing, and upgrades. The business ends when you cannot afford even the cheapest base permit, which is Canal Walk at $40.

### Condition

Every successful customer wears the unit down. At 0% condition, it is closed for the rest of the day. Customers who need it are turned away and each one produces a fine.

### Reputation

Reputation affects permit price and the price each customer pays. It also unlocks stronger locations. A bad day, especially one that ends with the unit closed, can damage it quickly.

### Traffic color

The map uses the following traffic tiers. Higher traffic brings more possible customers, but can also create more congestion and wear.

| Color | Tier | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue | Quiet | Lower foot traffic, safer early-game choice. |
| Green | Steady | Balanced traffic. |
| Gold | Busy | Stronger opportunity with more pressure. |
| Red | Crush | High-risk, high-volume traffic. |

## Locations

| Location | Traffic | Base permit | Reputation needed | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Canal Walk | Quiet | $40 | 0 | A low-cost starting site. |
| Pigeon Park | Steady | $55 | 0 | High need, balanced wear. |
| Market Lane | Busy | $75 | 30 | Shoppers linger and need the unit. |
| Office Row | Busy | $95 | 0 | Strong traffic with relatively low wear. |
| Station Steps | Crush | $125 | 55 | Premium commuters and high wear. |
| Weekend Festival | Crush | $225 | 65 | Friday/Saturday only; heavy demand and daily service requirement. |

The exact permit charged can be lower or higher than the base permit because of reputation.

## Upgrades

Open **Upgrades** from the planning map. Equipment improvements stay with the business across weeks. The Town Sign expires at the start of a new week.

| Upgrade | Cost | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Town Sign | $60 | Adds 30% more foot traffic for the current week. |
| Reinforced Structure | $85 | Reduces condition wear by 15%; adds $12 to each service. |
| Surge Pricing | $75 | Charges 10% more when a customer was turned away recently. |
| Air Freshener | $45 | Improves reputation gains and softens ordinary reputation losses. |
| Fan | $55 | Has the same reputation protection as the freshener; both stack. |

## Weekly structure

Days 1–7 form a week. Day 8 begins a new calendar while cash, condition, reputation, history, and permanent upgrades carry forward. The Town Sign resets. Before placing the unit on the first day of a new week, its condition must be at least 80%.

## Saving and restarting

The game saves automatically after meaningful state changes and during the operating day. Refreshing the page returns to the title screen, where **Continue** resumes the saved business. **New Game** replaces the saved business with a fresh start.
